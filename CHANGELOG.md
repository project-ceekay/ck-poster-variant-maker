# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Smooth window resizing: the window keeps up with the mouse while you drag its edge, and lays
  itself out once when you let go
- CK Software is credited as the publisher and copyright holder

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Turn one poster into a full-quality PNG, a web JPG and social posts (landscape 16:9, square,
  portrait 4:5 and story 9:16) in one click
- Social posts show the whole poster, sharp and centred, on a soft blurred copy of itself
- Output Sizes: change every variant's size, file name and quality, or switch it off
- Live preview of each social post before you generate
- Colours are converted to sRGB for web and social files, so they look right once posted
- Drag and drop, event-date folders, keyboard shortcuts (Ctrl+O, Ctrl+Enter)
- Built-in updates: checks GitHub once a day and installs new versions in one click
